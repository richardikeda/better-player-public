# Better Player

**[Português (Brasil)](../../README.md) · [English](../../languages/en/README.md) · [Other Languages](../../languages/README.md)**

Rekebisha kasi na sauti ya video za Instagram, rudia sehemu ulizochagua na pakua video zinazooana.

**[Sakinisha · Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/better-player/abapegnadkbbldnohmmbcklpicommlik)** · [Tovuti](https://richardikeda.github.io/better-player-public/languages/sw/) · [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/richardikeda/better-player-public/issues) · [Faragha (kwa Kireno)](https://richardikeda.github.io/better-player-public/#privacidade)

## Vipengele

- **Uchezaji na sauti**: sitisha, badilisha kasi, rekebisha sauti, buruta kalenda ya matukio, au ruka sekunde 5 nyuma au mbele.
- **Cheza tena kilicho muhimu**: fungua video nzima au uweke alama A na B ili kurudia sehemu hiyo pekee.
- **Njia zaidi za kutazama**: badilisha hadi skrini nzima au weka video kwenye dirisha la Picha-ndani-Picha linaloelea linapopatikana.
- **Ifanye iwe yako**: chagua kutoka mandhari 10, kutoka kwa kompakt Better Minimal hadi vidhibiti vikubwa vya Better XG. Weka wakati upau unapojificha na uwashe mikato ya kibodi ukipenda.
- **Uhifadhi wa video kwa hiari**: pakua faili za video zinazooana au rekodi klipu fupi ndani ya nchi inapotumika. Hifadhi tu maudhui ambayo una ruhusa ya kunakili.

## Mahali pa kusakinisha

Vivinjari vilivyo hapa chini vinaauni usakinishaji kupitia Duka la Wavuti la Chrome. Mchezaji Bora amejaribiwa kwenye Chrome na Edge kwenye Windows.

[Chrome](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/2664769) · [ChromeOS](https://support.google.com/chromebook/answer/2588006) · [Edge](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/edge/add-turn-off-or-remove-extensions-in-microsoft-edge) · [Brave](https://support.brave.app/hc/en-us/articles/360017909112-How-can-I-add-extensions-to-Brave) · [Vivaldi](https://help.vivaldi.com/desktop/appearance-customization/extensions/) · [Opera](https://help.opera.com/en/faq/) · [Yandex Browser](https://yandex.com/support/browser/en/personalization/extension) · [Dia](https://resources.arc.net/hc/en-us/articles/44413577819159-Arc-Sunset-Frequently-Asked-Questions)

**Android.** Ufungaji wa hati ya Vivaldi na Yandex Browser kwenye Android. Better Player bado inahitaji majaribio kwenye vivinjari hivi vya rununu. Katika Vivaldi, wezesha Tovuti ya Eneo-kazi. [Vivaldi](https://help.vivaldi.com/android/android-tools/extensions-on-android/) · [Yandex Browser](https://yandex.com/support/browser-mobile-android-phone/en/personal-settings/extensions)

Firefox na Safari hutumia njia tofauti za usambazaji. Toleo la Firefox liko kwenye majaribio.

## Msaada

Baada ya kusakinisha, fungua Instagram kwenye kivinjari chako. Bofya ikoni ya Kicheza Bora ili kusanidi vidhibiti. Unaporipoti tatizo, jumuisha kivinjari chako, toleo na hatua za kulizalisha tena.
